# Code of conduct

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, evaluate work on its merits, and respect each other’s privacy. Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing someone’s private information are unacceptable.

Maintain the confidentiality of client materials. Use fictional or redacted examples in issues, discussions, and pull requests.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Use the [security policy](SECURITY.md) for credential or data exposure. Maintainers may remove harmful material and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors.
